Priority is shape, not colour

Three tiers, monochrome

Fill, border and a small glyph carry the meaning, so it works for colour-blind users and survives a greyscale screenshot.

Colour proportion

90 / 9 / 1

Warm black and bone do almost everything. Semantic hues are small. Ember is a single live dot, ring, or hairline.

Photo scrim & lamp wash

Light only where it's warm

A vertical scrim guarantees type contrast on photos; a faint ember wash at the top of each screen is the lamp.

Reduced motion

Calm by default

Honour prefers-reduced-motion and an in-app toggle. Springs become 200 ms fades, travel and parallax are removed, loops stop, and nothing is conveyed by motion alone. Every drag has a button path; every moment has a story with reduce on.

<MotionConfig reducedMotion="user">
useCalm() → {
  spring: { duration: .2 },
  distance: 0, stagger: 0
}
06Alternate direction & sourcing

Paper Letter, and the toolbox

Paper Letter is the light-first alternate: printed-dossier calm, almost no motion. It ships as the Light theme of the same tokens. Components come from shadcn and Motion first; spectacle is rationed.
